A bicycle safety system that detects approaching cars with computer vision. An OAK-D depth camera and Raspberry Pi run a DepthAI/OpenCV model that estimates each vehicle's direction and distance, warning the rider through directional LEDs and audio alerts. An emergency button texts the rider's live location to a preset contact via Twilio, and ride data flows to a React/NestJS/MySQL app on GCP.

Won **Most Technically Challenging Hack** at HackDavis 2022.
