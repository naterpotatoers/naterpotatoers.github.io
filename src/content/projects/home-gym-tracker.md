A training app for a household garage gym. Users build multi-week programs with a live muscle-coverage preview, run workouts with every set prefilled from their last performance, and log several people training at once from one shared phone. Workouts sync to a Next.js app on Vercel backed by Supabase, where progress charts track estimated 1RMs and PRs and a front/back muscle heat map shows training intensity by day, week, or program.
