An IoT water bottle that measures how much water is left — and how much you have actually drunk over time — using an ESP32 with an ultrasonic depth sensor and an IMU. Readings stream to a React/Node/MySQL stack deployed on GCP through a CI/CD pipeline, where a dashboard lets users set hydration goals and compete on leaderboards.

Won **Best Health Hack**, **MLH's Best Hardware Hack**, and **QB3's Best Big Data for the Improvement of Health Care** at CruzHacks 2022.
