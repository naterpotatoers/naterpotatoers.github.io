An autonomous quadruped robot and scout drone built for AMD's Pervasive AI Contest 2024. The robot runs ROS2 on a Raspberry Pi, fusing GPS and IMU data for localization with gamepad control as a fallback. A React/TypeScript mission-control map built on Leaflet lets operators draw a search perimeter, place markers, track explored areas, and send waypoints to both the robot and the drone through an Express API.
